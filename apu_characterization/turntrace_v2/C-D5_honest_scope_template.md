## What this experiment establishes

Within the tested scripted-with-real-payload suite, models, deployments,
harnesses, seeds, and context ranges, the paired intervention measures whether
software orchestration discipline causally changes redundant-prefill cost. It
reports the recovered fraction, ideal-bound capture, and the measured
orchestration exchange rate.

## Fixed limitations

1. **Scripted, not open-agent behavior.** Tool sequences are fixed to keep A/B
   steps equivalent. The result does not establish the same recovery in an
   unconstrained autonomous trajectory.
2. **Suite-specific.** TT-EDIT, TT-RET, TT-FAN, TT-DOC, and TT-CHAIN are the
   claim domain. No generality beyond tested models, harnesses, deployments,
   context ranges, and seeds is asserted.
3. **Provider cache is reported, not controlled.** Cloud `cached_tokens` is a
   provider report. It is cross-checked against the structural LCP under the
   frozen OA-01 reconciliation envelope, but internal cache placement and
   serialization remain hidden.
4. **CPU pre-box is provisional forever.** CPU0 demonstrates mechanism and
   pipeline operation only. It is never a headline local-performance result.
5. **Conservative booking.** The fixed f(n) intercept is booked to necessary
   prefill, biasing against the thesis. The paired A/B delta survives that
   common booking by construction.
6. **Quality means the registered parity metric.** Exact success equality for
   deterministic scripted tasks is not a universal losslessness result.
7. **Arm B is software.** Its recovery and residual ideal-bound gap are not
   evidence of an APU or any hardware advantage.
8. **Energy denominator.** J/J is reported only when the box sidecar isolates
   Arm B's added orchestration energy. `joules_total` alone is insufficient.

## Null and negative outcomes

Provider/engine defaults may already recover nearly all stable-prefix work.
Arm A approximately equaling Arm B is the pre-registered null and banks
`rung_3c`; it is not relabeled as a tooling failure. Any parity failure also
banks `rung_3c` for the affected claim unless a documented implementation bug
is fixed and the entire affected cell is rerun.

## Measurement protocol

1. Timer correctness tests must pass first (python -m apu_characterization.tests.test_instr).
2. Warm state (tokenizer, corpus, vector matrix) loads before the measured window.
3. Per region: thread_time_ns (CPU, excludes I/O wait) and perf_counter_ns (wall), exclusive nesting.
4. Invariant per run: sum(category CPU) + residual = total thread CPU; residual must stay below 15%.
5. Instrumentation overhead: timer microbenchmark plus APU_NOINSTR=1 comparison run; must stay below 3% or be mitigated.
6. py-spy sampling cross-check at c=100 mixed; agreement within 10 percentage points per major category.
7. Medians and IQR over 5 seeds minimum; no single-run numbers in figures or tables.
